- ✅ npm install
- ✅ npm run build
- ✅ npm run preview
- ✅ Lighthouse review
- ✅ Mobile testing
- ✅ Tablet testing
- ✅ Desktop testing
- ✅ Contact form configured and tested
- ✅ Sitemap available
- ✅ Robots available
- ✅ SEO metadata checked
- ✅ Structured data checked
- ✅ Images optimized and verified
- ✅ Favicons verified
- ✅ SSL enabled

## Manual business-content confirmations before launch

- Confirm official business name
- Confirm phone and email
- Confirm address and maps link
- Confirm licensing authority and number
- Confirm policy text and effective dates
- Confirm final program availability language
